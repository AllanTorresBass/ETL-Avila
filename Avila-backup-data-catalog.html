The folder is the billing archive of G.P.S. Gestión Profesional de Salud Avila, C.A., a medical provider in Valera, Trujillo. It holds the paperwork and registers used to request, authorize, deliver, and invoice care for insurers and corporate accounts.

This catalog describes structure. It does not list patients, identity numbers, diagnoses, or phone numbers. Those values are inside the files.

The billing cycle the names describe

  1. Request — cintillo, solicitud, AMP servicio inmediato, clave workbook.
  2. Authorization — carta aval, clave RMS, the authorization name on a relación.
  3. Clinical packet — scanner pages, ingreso, egreso, informe, verificación, exam photos.
  4. Invoice — FACT plus a number, sometimes with the patient name in the file name.
  5. Batch register — the sábana or relación for that month and lot.
  6. Price list — a baremo. These sit at the root and are reference data, not cases.

6.8 Invoice and supply templates

FORMATO INSUMOS DE HOSPITALIZACION….xlsx is a template book of 24 sheets, one layout per client (UVM, Haz Médica, Palpitar, Cemento Andino, Latitud, Cámara Municipal, Seguros Miranda, Previsora, and others). The same book is copied into CORPOELEC/PRESUPUESTO CORPOELEC.xlsx, CASOS INAC/FORMATO FACTURA.xlsx, and PREVISORA/FORMATO FACTURA.xlsx.

Line fields: system code, service, quantity, unit price, net total, payment method, bolívar amount, titular, beneficiary, cédula, client code, invoice number, issue date, subtotal, IVA 16%, total. Service-code prefixes in the template: CN- consult LB- lab RX- X-ray PE- exam package US- ultrasound. One sheet is a materials list (tegaderm, gauze, syringes, and similar). CORPOELEC also has 13 per-person budget workbooks and hundreds of budget PDFs.

What a loader has to expect

  • Many dates are Excel serials. Folder “years” such as 2043 are invoice numbers that start with 20. The calendar years in folder names are 2024, 2025, and 2026.
  • The same invoice is often a PDF, a JPG, and a slide, and also a row in a sábana.
  • The two .rar files duplicate October 2026 scanner lots that are already expanded as PDFs.
  • Scanner pages named only Escáner_… (n) have no case key in the file name. They belong to the batch folder and have to be matched by OCR or by scan order.
  • Scanner PDFs and PowerPoint-export PDFs have no text layer.
  • CARPETA INGENIERO is tax and legal material (ISLR, lease, acta constitutiva). It stays out of the clinical model.
  • Shortcuts, desktop.ini, and the empty .tmp are not records.
